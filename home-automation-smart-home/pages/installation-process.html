FAQ

Installation Process Questions

Everything you need to know before we transform your home. Still curious?

How long does the entire installation process take?
A typical installation spans 1 to 3 weeks from consultation to handover. The timeline depends on the size of your home and the number of devices. The on-site installation itself usually takes 2 to 7 days, and we work around your schedule to minimize disruption.
Will the installation disrupt my daily routine or damage my home?
No. We use non-invasive techniques wherever possible, complete most work room-by-room, and thoroughly protect floors and furniture. Any minor drilling or rewiring is planned in advance, agreed with you, and fully restored and cleaned before we leave.
Can existing homes be retrofitted, or does it require new construction?
Absolutely. Over 70% of our installations are retrofits in existing homes. Our wireless-first approach means most devices work over Wi-Fi, Zigbee, and Z-Wave with no structural changes. For best results, tell us early if you're planning new construction.
What happens after installation? Do you offer aftercare?
Every installation includes a full handover with on-site training and documentation. You also get warranty coverage on all devices, complimentary remote support for the first year, and optional monitoring and maintenance plans to keep everything in top shape.
Do I need good Wi-Fi or a smart hub for the system to work?
We assess your network during the site visit and recommend an upgraded router or a dedicated smart hub if needed. Your devices will work offline for core functions like lighting and security, with remote access available over the internet.
Can I add more devices or rooms later?
Yes. Our design is open and scalable, so you can add devices, rooms, or even a second home at any time. Existing automations and control panels update automatically when you expand your setup.
