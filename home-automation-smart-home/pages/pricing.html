FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, installation, and what's included before you get started.

How much does a Nexora Home smart home setup cost?
Our packages start at ₹49,999 (Essential), with our most popular Connect plan at ₹99,999 and the complete Signature package at ₹1,99,999. Final pricing depends on home size, device count, and the level of automation you choose.
Is installation included in the price?
Standard installation is included in all packages. The Essential plan includes a standard install, while Connect and Signature include full professional installation and configuration of every device.
How long does installation take?
Most installations are completed within 2 to 5 days, depending on the package and home size. Signature setups with motorized curtains and whole-home systems may take up to a week for full calibration.
What warranty do you offer on the products?
Every package includes a hardware warranty: 1 year with Essential, 2 years with Connect, and 3 years with Signature. Extended warranty plans are available for purchase on any package.
Can I customize my package or add extra devices?
Absolutely. Every package can be tailored to your home. Use our custom quote option to add or remove devices, extend coverage to more rooms, or integrate special requirements like pool or home theatre controls.
Do you offer payment plans or EMI?
Yes. We offer flexible payment options including no-cost EMI for 6 to 24 months, split payments across the install timeline (50% advance, 40% on installation day, 10% after handover), and corporate billing for businesses.
What's included in each package?
Each package includes the smart devices listed for that tier, the Nexora Home app with full mobile control, professional installation, setup and training, plus ongoing technical support. Our comparison table above gives you a complete itemized breakdown.
