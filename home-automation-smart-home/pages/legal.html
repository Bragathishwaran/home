Privacy, Terms & Cookie Policy

Transparency matters. Here's how Nexora Home collects, uses and protects your information — and the terms that govern your use of our services.

Last updated: September 11, 2026. These policies apply to nexorahome.com and all Nexora Home services.

Privacy Policy

Nexora Home ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, request a quote, or use our smart home products and apps, and how we protect it.

Information we collect:

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, phone number, address) when requesting a consultation or quote.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device, pages visited and cookies (see Cookie Policy below).
  • Smart home usage data from the devices and apps you connect, used only to operate and improve your system.

How we use your information:

  • To respond to enquiries, schedule installations and provide support.
  • To operate, secure and improve our smart home platform and products.
  • To send you product updates and offers — only if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Data protection: We use industry-standard encryption and access controls. Your data is never sold to third parties. We only share information with trusted service providers who help us deliver our services and are bound by confidentiality.

Your rights: You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at nexorahome.com/pages/contact.html.

By accessing nexorahome.com, purchasing products, or requesting our installation services, you agree to the following terms.

  • All pricing shown on this website is indicative and may vary based on your home's wiring, size and configuration. Final quotes are confirmed after a site survey.
  • Hardware products carry a manufacturer warranty, and installations carry a written workmanship guarantee. Warranty terms are provided at the time of purchase.
  • Installed systems rely on your internet connection and power supply; Nexora Home is not responsible for outages caused by third-party service providers.
  • Software, apps and cloud services are provided on an "as is" basis and may be updated from time to time to improve security and functionality.
  • You agree not to misuse the website, attempt unauthorized access, or use our services for any unlawful purpose.
  • Nexora Home reserves the right to update these terms. Material changes will be communicated through the website.

Quoted installation timelines are estimates. Our team will keep you informed of any schedule changes and will never start work without a confirmed agreement.

Cookie Policy

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to make the experience faster, more secure and more useful to you.

What we use:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function (for example, remembering your theme preference).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These are anonymized where possible.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices you make, such as language or region.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking essential cookies may affect parts of the site, but most content will remain accessible.
